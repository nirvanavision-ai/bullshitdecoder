How to read anything here

It describes text, not people

No output concludes that anyone is an abuser. A flagged phrase is a pattern that appears in the words, nothing more. Intent lives in a person; these tools only ever see a sentence.

Mitigating evidence counts

Apologies, concessions, and hedging genuinely lower the reading. An instrument that can only escalate isn't an instrument — it's a mirror for whatever you brought to it.

A clear result is not absolution

Harm travels in ways no text analyser catches: tone, history, timing, the thing left unsaid. If something felt wrong and nothing here flags it, your read still counts as data.

Every claim shows its source

Each pattern carries its lineage — Freyd on DARVO, Stark on coercive control, Sweet on gaslighting, Bancroft on entitlement — shown to you verbatim, so you can check the work.

An educational instrument. Not a diagnosis, not a psychological assessment, not evidence about any person's character or intent.
